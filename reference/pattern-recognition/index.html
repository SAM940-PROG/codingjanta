What should I think about?

Answer honestly — the output suggests candidate families to confirm with signals and red flags, not keyword matches.

Red flags per family

  • Binary Search: no sortedness or monotonicity.
  • Sliding Window: validity cannot be maintained incrementally (e.g. negatives in sum windows).
  • Greedy: no exchange-argument justification — test against brute force on small cases.
  • BFS: weighted edges with varying positive costs — use Dijkstra.
  • Prefix sums: interleaved point updates — use Fenwick/segment tree.
  • HashMap: order or neighbors matter — sort first.
  • DSU: deletions/splits in forward time — needs offline reversal or another structure.
