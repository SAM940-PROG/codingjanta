WeekTopicsHoursMilestone
1Big-O, Arrays, Strings, Java basics7–10hComfortable with complexity notation

6Linked Lists, Stacks, Monotonic Stacks, Queues8–12hUnderstand how structures create patterns
7Trees, BST, Heaps, Tries8–12hNavigate hierarchical and heap-based problems
8Graphs: BFS, DFS, components, cycles, topo sort10–14hSee graphs as nodes + edges + traversal
9Dijkstra, MST, Union-Find10–14hMatch algorithm to problem type
10Greedy, Bit Manipulation, Math10–14hApply contest tricks and optimization
11DP: 1D, 2D, Knapsack, Interval, Bitmask12–16hIdentify DP state and transition
12Segment Trees, Fenwick, Advanced Topics12–16hKnow what exists; long-term specialization

⚠ Important

Week 12 is an introduction to advanced techniques, not mastery. These topics are for long-term specialization. Don't feel pressure to fully master every advanced topic in 12 weeks — the guide remains a reference for years to come.

One curriculum, two speeds: learn the idea step by step, then use the reference at full speed. Your progress stays on this device.
