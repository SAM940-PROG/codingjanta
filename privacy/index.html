Privacy

This site does not require accounts and does not collect personal information.

What stays on your device

Lesson completion, checklist state, quiz and practice scores, and UI preferences (theme, sidebar) are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us, because there is no backend.

What we do not collect

No names, emails, passwords, or payment details. No user accounts exist.

Third parties

Outbound links lead to third-party websites (for example, online judges) governed by their own privacy policies. No analytics or advertising is currently active on this site. If that ever changes, this page will be updated before anything is enabled.

Removing local data

Clearing your browser's site data (or using the in-page reset controls where offered) removes all locally stored progress.

One curriculum, two speeds: learn the idea step by step, then use the reference at full speed. Your progress stays on this device.
